## Design Context

### Users

Musicians and producers working in a focused desktop session. They use YES
Stems to separate, audition, inspect, and export stems without sending audio to
a remote service.

### Brand Personality

Precise, confident, studio-grade. YES Stems belongs to the same professional
audio suite as YES Master, while remaining the more spectral, instrument-aware
member of the family.

### Aesthetic Direction

A dense dark pro-audio workstation. YES Audio family chrome uses cool graphite
surfaces, cobalt-blue interaction states, compact typography, hairline borders,
and top-lit controls. YES Stems keeps its own identity through sea-glass signal
visualization, multicolor instrument stems, and a numbered extraction workflow.

### Design Principles

- Blue means suite navigation, selection, and primary action.
- Teal means source signal, analysis, and local extraction—not every control.
- Preserve the one-screen extraction workstation and its information density.
- Keep instrument colors functional and stable across preview, meters, and
  audition controls.
- Prefer restrained depth, exact state feedback, and accessible contrast over
  decorative effects.
